## Featured project: Study Time and Mathematics Grades

**Research question:** Is weekly study time associated with students’ final mathematics grades?

**Status:** Completed

The analysis of 395 students found a weak positive association between weekly study-time category and final grade (Spearman correlation = 0.105). This is an association, not proof that studying more causes higher grades.
